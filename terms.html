3. Stamps, accuracy and evidence

GeoProof records the time from your phone's clock and the location from your phone's positioning services. Please understand that:

  • GPS accuracy varies, and is often worse indoors, near tall buildings or with approximate location. The app shows the reported accuracy, but the real position can be outside it.
  • A phone's clock and location can be wrong, changed by the user or faked by other software. The app labels stale, approximate, missing and imported data, but it cannot detect every manipulation.
  • Stamps and reports are visible annotations and documentation aids. They are not certified, notarised or cryptographic proof of when or where a photo was taken, and they do not replace a professional survey, inspection certificate or legal advice.
  • Whether a court, insurer, client or authority accepts GeoProof records is up to them. We make no promise that they will.
  • Addresses from the optional lookup are approximate and come from a third-party geocoding service.
