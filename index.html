Honest by design

A stamp is only useful if people can trust it. GeoProof never fills gaps with guesses.

  • No location fix? The photo says “Location unavailable”. Coordinates are never invented.
  • Old or inaccurate fix? It's labelled, for example “GPS fix 3 min before capture”.
  • Capture time and GPS fix time are recorded separately, with the time zone.
  • Imported photos are marked imported, and their metadata is marked unverified.
  • Reports list every caveat in a disclosures section.
