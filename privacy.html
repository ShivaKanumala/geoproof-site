Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Version 1.1

This policy explains how the GeoProof Android app (“the app”, package com.geoproof.fieldcamera) and this website handle information. The app is published by Siva Prasad (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, write to sivaprasad979@gmail.com.

The short version
  • Your photos, locations, notes, projects and reports are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • We run no servers that receive your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The app is free.
  • Location is used only while the app is on screen, never in the background.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you share it, or when you turn on the optional address lookup.
  • Stamped photos you share contain their GPS location by default. You can turn this off in Settings.

The app creates and stores the following on your device. None of it is sent to us.

The app records a SHA-256 fingerprint of each original photo so you can check later that the file has not changed. The app does not collect contacts, call logs, microphone audio, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

2. Location

  • The app asks for location permission so it can record where photos are taken. You can choose precise or approximate location. Approximate locations are clearly labelled.
  • Location is read only while the camera screen, the Home screen's GPS status card or a “use current location” action is on screen. The app does not request background location and does not track you.
  • You can refuse location access and still take photos. They are then marked “Location unavailable”. The app never invents or estimates coordinates.
  • Compass heading comes from your phone's motion sensors and is labelled magnetic and uncalibrated.

3. Camera and photos

Camera permission is used only to take photos inside the app. The app does not record video or audio, and the microphone permission is removed from the app. To import an existing photo you pick it with Android's system photo picker, so the app can only see the photos you select. It reads their date and location metadata, which is labelled “unverified”.

4. Optional address lookup

Address lookup is off by default. If you turn it on, the coordinates of your photos are sent to your phone's built-in geocoding service to get a street address. On most Android phones this service is provided by Google, and Google's privacy policy applies to that request. GeoProof does not send your photos. Results are cached on your phone so nearby photos don't need another lookup. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

5. When data leaves your phone

Your data leaves your phone only in these cases, all started by you:

  • Sharing photos or PDF reports through Android's share sheet (for example to WhatsApp or email). The receiving app or person gets the file, and their own privacy terms apply.
  • Address lookup, if you turn it on (section 4).
Shared photos can reveal location. Stamped copies show the time and coordinates on the image, and by default also carry the GPS location in their EXIF metadata. You can switch off “Write GPS into stamped copies (EXIF)” in Settings. Originals are shared exactly as the camera saved them, including any metadata the camera wrote. PDF reports include the times, coordinates and addresses of their photos. Think about who will receive a file before you share it.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We never receive it in the first place.

6. Storage, backup and deletion

  • All app data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for GeoProof data, so your photos and locations are not copied to your Google account. This also means the app cannot restore them for you. Share or copy anything you need to keep before you change or reset your phone.
  • Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a photo, project or report in the app removes its files. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all GeoProof data on the phone.
  • Because we hold none of your data, we cannot recover or delete anything for you remotely.

7. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's own encryption and screen lock. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see your photos, so use a screen lock. Files are shared through temporary content links, never through private file paths. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but keeping data on your device and off any server limits who can reach it.

9. Children

GeoProof is a work tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. In any case, the app does not send personal information to us.

10. Your choices and rights

You control your data directly in the app. You can view, edit, share or delete any photo, project or report, turn address lookup and GPS-in-EXIF on or off, and revoke camera or location permission in Android settings at any time. Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the EU GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data. Because we do not hold your data, you can exercise those rights in the app itself. For any request or concern, contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

11. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party fonts or scripts. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
